About All Property Repairs

A trade practiced
as a craft.

The Story

Built on one principle: do it all, do it properly.

All Property Repairs was founded on a simple frustration shared by homeowners across Adelaide — hiring a plasterer, then waiting on a cornice installer, then coordinating a painter, all for a single room. Delays compound. Standards vary. No one takes full ownership of the result.

Andreas built his business to solve exactly that. Fully licensed and experienced across plasterboard, flushing, cornice and painting, he takes a project from the first sheet of Gyprock to the final coat of paint personally — the same hands, the same standard, throughout.

The Approach

Why one tradesman, start to finish.

Removing the handoffs between trades doesn't just save time — it protects the quality of the finish. Every decision, from how a sheet is fixed to how a cornice miters into a corner, is made by the same person who will paint over it.

01

Complete accountability

One tradesman means one point of contact and one standard held from start to finish — no finger-pointing between trades.

02

No scheduling gaps

Work progresses continuously through each stage, instead of waiting weeks between separately booked contractors.

03

Precision at every stage

Flushing informs cornice placement; cornice placement informs the paint line. Continuity produces a cleaner result.
