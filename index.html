The Philosophy

No subcontractors. No coordination hassle. Just one craftsman.

Most room finishing projects pass through several sets of hands — a plasterer, a cornice installer, a painter — each with their own schedule, standards and margin for miscommunication. All Property Repairs was built to remove that friction entirely.

Andreas manages your project personally, from plasterboard through to the final coat of paint, so the work reads as one continuous, considered process rather than four disconnected jobs.
